01 No Context Awareness

AI tools generate from scratch — they never look at your past emails or learn how you actually write. Every draft sounds generic.

02 Zero Human Control

Most tools auto-send without approval. No editing, no preview, no way to catch mistakes before they reach the recipient.

03 No Recipient Intelligence

Knowing who you're writing to matters. No existing tool researches the recipient's company, role, or your history with them.

04 Tone Mismatch

You write differently to your boss vs. a friend. Generic AI outputs miss the nuance of established relationships.

05 — The Brain Behind It
RAG that remembers.

Mail-AI uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation to make every email personal. Every sent email is automatically embedded into 768-dimensional vectors using Gemini's embedding model and stored in pgvector (PostgreSQL). When drafting a new email, the Writer agent performs cosine similarity search to retrieve the most relevant past emails — matching tone, structure, and context.

01

Semantic Thread Search

Finds the 3 most semantically similar past emails to the same recipient using pgvector cosine distance. Surfaces related conversations for context.

Input: User's brief + recipient email
Process: Embed the brief, compute cosine distance
Output: Top 3 semantically related past emails
Used for: Conversation continuity & context
02

Template Reuse

Finds a structurally similar past email you've sent — reuses it as a style/structure template. Triggered when the user asks to "mirror" a previous email.

Input: Brief description of desired email
Process: Find closest structural match via embedding
Output: 1 most similar past email as template
Used for: Style & structure consistency
03

Recipient Tone Match

Pulls the last 5 emails to a specific recipient. Extracts the established tone (formal, friendly, etc.) so the Writer agent matches your voice with that person.

Input: Recipient email address
Process: Query last 5 emails to that person
Output: Extracted tone (formal, friendly, casual...)
Used for: Per-recipient tone matching

What I Learned

RAG & Vector Search

Building a full embedding pipeline — generate, store, retrieve. pgvector's cosine distance operator makes semantic search dead simple in PostgreSQL.

Multi-Agent Orchestration

Coordinating specialized agents with different tools and schemas — decomposing complex AI tasks into composable units.

OAuth & Token Management

Gmail OAuth with refresh tokens, secure storage, and automatic renewal. Deep understanding of modern auth flows.

Human-Centered AI Design

AI augments judgment, never replaces it. Every design decision reinforces user control and transparency.
